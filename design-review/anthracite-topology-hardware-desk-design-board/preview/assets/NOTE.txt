preview/assets/
=========================

Intentionally near-empty.

The Topology Hardware Desk is parameter-driven: every glyph, primitive,
and faceplate is inline SVG inside ../../source/*.jsx. There are no
bitmaps, sprites, or font files to ship.

PNG snapshots of the sheets live in ../../sheets/, not here.
This folder may be deleted by OCC once the no-bitmap rule is internalised.
