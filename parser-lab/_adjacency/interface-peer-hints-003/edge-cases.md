# Interface peer hints edge cases

- Descriptions may be stale, abbreviated, or intentionally vague.
- The same peer-like label may appear on several interfaces.
- A routed uplink does not imply a unique neighbor identity.
- L2 access ports can still point at device-internal or virtual targets.
- SR OS and IOS-XR interface naming can encode role, but that role is still a
  hint.
- Do not promote naming conventions into edges without discovery evidence.
